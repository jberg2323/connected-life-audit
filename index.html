The Connected Life Audit

Eight short stops, about 15 minutes. You leave with one page: what matters to you, who matters to you, and what you’ll do about it this week. Built on the ideas in Lisa Kalfus’s essay on what 50 years taught her.

    Your answers stay in this browser and nobody else can see them. The ideas come from Lisa Kalfus’s essay “What 50 Years Taught Me About Living Fully Connected”. The exercises and wording here are an independent adaptation, so read her original for the stories behind them.
